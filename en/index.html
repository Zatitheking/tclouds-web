Our product

Anyway — business management you start with one module

One system, six modules. You don't have to buy them all, and you don't have to roll them out at once: start with whatever hurts most and add the rest later. Built for Hungarian regulatory compliance, connected to the tax authority's online invoicing, hosted in the cloud.

  1. 01

    Accounting

    For accounting firms: client companies, documents, tax authority and bank connections, monthly close and missing-item lists in one place.

  2. 02

    Finance

    A company's own financial picture, no accountant required: receivables, expected income and expenses, cash-flow outlook.

  3. 03

    CRM

    Company records, deals, sales stages, quotes and automations — from first contact to signed contract.

  4. 04

    HR

    Employee records, employment relationships, registration forms, leave tracking, job postings and applicants.

  5. 05

    Legal

    Contract repository with templates, electronic signatures, expiry and notice-period monitoring, filing.

  6. 06

    Marketing

    Campaigns, recipient lists that refresh automatically from the other modules, sending and measurement, lead handling.

Services

Custom development

Not every process fits into an off-the-shelf product. We start where existing software ends: integrating it, extending it, or writing what is missing.

Anyway wasn't born in a product design workshop — it was born because we needed it ourselves. We bring the same approach to client work: understand the process first, write code second. Not the other way around.

We are a small team, so we don't take on everything. What we do take on, we finish — and then we keep it running.

Let's talk
  • Business web applications Internal systems, client portals, operational interfaces — from scratch or alongside an existing system.
  • System integration Tax authority online invoicing, banking data feeds, company registries, invoicing and payroll systems connected together.
  • Process automation Replacing repetitive admin: document processing, notifications, reports, scheduled jobs.
  • Mobile applications iOS and Android apps alongside existing systems, from a shared codebase.
  • AI solutions Data extraction from documents, text processing, decision support — where it actually pays off.

New direction · 2026

Game development

In 2026 we started our own game development branch. Our first project is a turn-based strategy game for PC, with a console release planned further out.

In development

The same engineering approach as in our business software: the game's logic core is built as engine-independent, independently testable code, with rendering layered on top. That keeps the system maintainable long term and avoids locking it to a single technology.

Development happens on PC; target platforms are tested on real console hardware. We'll talk about a release when there is something to show.

  • Genre Turn-based strategy — city building, population, economy and army management.
  • Technology Unity, C#. The simulation core lives in a separate, engine-independent library with automated tests.
  • Platform PC first. Console release in a later phase of development, tested on target hardware.
  • Status Prototype phase. No release date announced.
